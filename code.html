The Art of Infusion

Sprite Cool is born from a desire to elevate the everyday beverage. We start with the highest quality milk, sourced from responsible dairy farms.

Through a proprietary cold-steeping process, we introduce the essence of damask roses, capturing their most delicate aromatic notes without any artificial bitterness. The result is a creamy, perfectly balanced drink that refreshes the body and calms the mind.
